THE LITTLE DETAILS

Good to know.

No account. No download.
Just a little everyday play.

How do I install the home-screen app?

Use Add to home screen above the game for instructions. Open the app online and wait for “Ready for offline play” before going offline. Your browser can remove cached files when storage is low, so reconnect if offline play stops working.

How do I build a bigger stack?

Wait until your moving character is centered over the platform or the squishy below it, then tap Drop a Squish. Each new character starts away from your target. As your score grows, characters move faster and landings need to be more precise.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. Tap the drop button or the play area. On a computer, you can also use the spacebar. Use the pause button for a break and the circular arrow for a fresh round.

Does the app save my best score?

Your best score and sound preference are saved on this device when browser storage is available. They stay after closing the app, but clearing site data removes them. Scores do not sync between devices or browsers.

Can I buy the characters in the game?

Pip, Splash, Butter, Twirl, Sprout, and Berry are original digital characters. The shopping links help you explore similar toy styles, but those products are not exact copies of our characters. Squish Stack is independent and is not affiliated with Schylling or NeeDoh.
